## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the proposed subscription and revenue strategy for the Revolving Bible platform.

The objective is to develop a sustainable commercial product that serves both individual Christians and churches while keeping essential Scripture features accessible.

The strategy will guide the development of subscription plans, premium features, payment processing, customer management, and future pricing decisions.

No subscription prices are finalized by this document.

## 2. Business Model

The proposed business model combines:

1. A free individual plan.
2. A premium individual subscription.
3. A limited free church plan.
4. A premium church subscription.

This approach allows users to experience the platform before deciding whether to purchase additional features.

The business model should support recurring revenue while maintaining the platform's core purpose of making Scripture accessible.

## 3. Target Customers

The initial target market consists of two customer groups.

### 3.1 Individual Users

Individual Christians who want to:

- Read daily Scripture.
- Use automatic Scripture rotation.
- Save favourite passages.
- Share Bible passages.
- Receive optional reminders.
- Access additional personal Bible features.

### 3.2 Churches and Ministries

Churches and ministries that want to:

- Display Scripture during services.
- Synchronize multiple display screens.
- Allow preachers to control readings.
- Share Scripture through QR codes.
- Manage church users.
- Schedule Scripture selections.
- Operate a centralized Scripture display system.

## 4. Proposed Subscription Plans

### 4.1 Individual Free Plan

The free plan will introduce users to the main Scripture experience.

Proposed features:

- Access to the approved Scripture library.
- Daily Scripture presentation.
- Basic automatic Scripture rotation.
- Basic Scripture sharing.
- Standard application display.

The free plan should remain useful rather than functioning only as a temporary demonstration.

### 4.2 Individual Premium Plan

The premium plan will provide additional personal features.

Proposed features:

- Expanded personal reading tools.
- Unlimited or increased favourites.
- Advanced reading preferences.
- Custom rotation settings.
- Additional Scripture collections.
- Optional reminders and notifications.
- Reading history, where enabled.
- Additional personalization features.

The final feature list will depend on development costs and user demand.

### 4.3 Church Free Plan

The free church plan will allow smaller churches to experience the display system.

Possible features:

- One church account.
- Basic Scripture display.
- Basic automatic rotation.
- Preacher-controlled reading.
- QR-code Scripture sharing.
- Limited administration functions.

Usage limits may apply to the number of registered displays or authorized users.

### 4.4 Church Premium Plan

The premium church plan will provide more advanced church management capabilities.

Proposed features:

- Multiple synchronized display screens.
- Advanced display management.
- Multiple authorized church users.
- Service scheduling.
- Weekly Scripture selections.
- Expanded church administration.
- Advanced rotation settings.
- Additional display layouts.
- Priority technical support.
- Future reporting and management tools.

The final limits and features will be determined during commercial development.

## 5. Proposed Pricing Structure

The platform should support monthly and annual subscriptions where appropriate.

Pricing must be assessed against:

- Target customer purchasing power.
- Supabase and hosting costs.
- Application maintenance.
- Payment processing charges.
- App store commissions.
- Technical support costs.
- Development and operational expenses.
- Competitor pricing.
- Expected customer growth.

The project should consider both Jamaican and international customers.

Prices should be displayed in the appropriate currency where supported.

No final prices should be published until the financial model has been reviewed.

## 6. Jamaican and International Markets

The platform will initially target both local and international users.

Pricing should consider:

- Jamaican Dollar (JMD).
- United States Dollar (USD).
- Other supported currencies as the business expands.

The project should avoid assuming that one pricing structure will suit every market.

Regional pricing may be considered after evaluating payment provider capabilities, taxes, and market demand.

## 7. Subscription Management

The commercial platform should provide secure subscription management.

Users should be able to:

- View their current plan.
- Review available plans.
- Upgrade their subscription.
- Downgrade where supported.
- Cancel recurring payments.
- View renewal information.
- Review payment status.
- Access relevant billing support.

Changes to subscription status must be synchronized with the backend.

## 8. Payment Processing

Payments should be handled through reputable payment providers or approved app store billing systems.

Potential payment channels may include:

- Website-based payment processing.
- Google Play Billing.
- Apple In-App Purchase.

The selected payment providers must be assessed for availability in Jamaica and the international markets being targeted.

The platform should retain only the payment information necessary for subscription management.

Payment card details should remain with the authorized payment provider unless a properly assessed payment arrangement requires otherwise.

## 9. Church Subscription Administration

Church subscriptions should be associated with the relevant church organization.

The system should support:

- Church subscription ownership.
- Authorized billing administrators.
- Subscription renewal tracking.
- Plan upgrades and downgrades.
- Payment status monitoring.
- Grace periods where appropriate.
- Suspension of premium features after applicable billing procedures.

A church's Scripture display should not be unnecessarily disrupted because of a temporary payment processing problem.

The project should establish a clear grace-period and service-continuity policy.

## 10. Free Trial Strategy

A free trial may be introduced for selected premium features.

Possible approaches include:

- A time-limited church premium trial.
- A limited individual premium trial.
- A demonstration account for prospective churches.

Trial conditions must be clearly disclosed.

The project must not automatically charge users after a trial unless the renewal terms have been clearly presented and properly accepted.

## 11. Revenue Streams

Potential revenue sources include:

1. Individual premium subscriptions.
2. Church premium subscriptions.
3. Annual subscription plans.
4. Additional church display services.
5. Future licensed content offerings, where appropriate.
6. Optional premium support services.

The project should avoid excessive commercialization of essential Scripture access.

## 12. Subscription Database Requirements

The commercial backend should support a subscription management structure.

The proposed subscription record should include:

- Subscription ID.
- User or church account ID.
- Plan identifier.
- Payment provider.
- Provider transaction reference.
- Subscription status.
- Start date.
- Renewal date.
- Cancellation date, where applicable.
- Currency.
- Applicable pricing reference.
- Created and updated timestamps.

Sensitive payment credentials must not be stored in the general application database.

Subscription records must be protected through appropriate access controls and Row Level Security.

## 13. Customer Support

Subscription customers should have access to support for:

- Payment problems.
- Failed renewals.
- Subscription cancellation.
- Plan changes.
- Account access.
- Billing questions.
- Technical issues affecting paid features.

The project should establish response expectations before commercial launch.

## 14. Financial Sustainability

Subscription revenue should cover the ongoing cost of operating and improving the platform.

The project should monitor:

- Monthly recurring revenue.
- Annual recurring revenue.
- Number of active subscriptions.
- Free-to-paid conversion.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Payment processing fees.
- Hosting and infrastructure expenses.
- Customer retention.
- Subscription cancellations.
- Net operating margin.

These measures will help determine whether the subscription model is sustainable.

## 15. Future Commercial Expansion

Future opportunities may include:

- Church network subscriptions.
- Multiple-location church management.
- Larger ministry organization plans.
- Advanced display packages.
- Additional licensed Bible translations.
- Specialized church administration features.
- Premium support arrangements.

Expansion must be based on customer demand and operational capacity.

## 16. Commercial Release Requirements

Before subscriptions are launched, confirm that:

- [ ] Free and premium features are clearly defined.
- [ ] Individual and church plans are implemented.
- [ ] Pricing has been reviewed.
- [ ] Payment providers have been selected.
- [ ] Google Play and Apple billing requirements have been assessed.
- [ ] Subscription renewal and cancellation procedures are implemented.
- [ ] Refund conditions are documented.
- [ ] Church billing permissions are established.
- [ ] Subscription security has been tested.
- [ ] Customer support procedures are established.
- [ ] Financial monitoring is available.
- [ ] Applicable tax and consumer protection obligations have been reviewed.

## 17. Review and Approval

This strategy is a proposed commercial framework.

Pricing, feature limits, payment providers, and subscription conditions remain subject to business planning, technical assessment, customer feedback, and legal review.
