# Branding and Intellectual Property Management Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes how the Revolving Bible platform's brand identity, original software, documentation, creative materials, and other intellectual property will be managed and protected.

The objective is to build a recognizable and trustworthy brand while reducing the risk of intellectual property disputes as the product develops into a commercial service.

## 2. Brand Identity

The working product name is:

**Revolving Bible**

The brand represents a digital Scripture platform designed to make Bible passages accessible through automatic rotation, individual reading, and church display technology.

The brand should communicate:

- Scripture accessibility.
- Simplicity.
- Reliability.
- Christian encouragement.
- Modern technology.
- Church and individual usability.

The visual identity should remain welcoming and broadly accessible to Christians across different denominations.

## 3. Brand Components

The project may develop the following brand assets:

- Product name.
- Logo.
- Application icon.
- Brand colours.
- Typography.
- Website design.
- Mobile application design.
- Promotional graphics.
- Marketing materials.
- Product descriptions and slogans.

All approved brand assets should be stored in the project repository or another controlled location.

## 4. Name and Trademark Review

The name Revolving Bible is currently a working product name.

Before commercial launch, a name and trademark review must establish whether the name is available and suitable for commercial use.

The review should consider:

- Existing trademarks.
- Similar product names.
- Mobile applications using similar names.
- Domain name availability.
- Social media username availability.
- Potential confusion with existing religious or technology products.

Trademark searches should cover Jamaica and the principal international markets in which the product is intended to operate.

A qualified intellectual property professional should review any significant uncertainty before registration or launch.

The project must not claim that the name is registered or legally protected unless registration has actually been completed.

## 5. Logo and Visual Identity Ownership

The project may commission or create an original logo and visual identity.

Before using externally created designs commercially, the project must establish:

- Who created the design.
- Whether the creator retains ownership.
- Whether commercial usage rights have been transferred or licensed.
- Whether modifications are permitted.
- Whether the design may be used in mobile applications and advertising.

Written agreements should be retained where appropriate.

## 6. Software and Source Code

The Revolving Bible source code should remain under controlled version management.

The project repository contains source code and technical documentation.

Access should be restricted to authorized contributors.

Future development agreements should clearly address:

- Ownership of newly written code.
- Rights to modify the application.
- Rights to distribute commercial versions.
- Contractor and developer contributions.
- Use of third-party libraries.
- Open-source licence obligations.
- Ownership of custom application designs.

The project should maintain a clear record of who has contributed significant original work.

## 7. Original Written Content

Original materials may include:

- Scripture-related reflections.
- Application descriptions.
- User guides.
- Marketing text.
- Educational material.
- Original illustrations.
- Product documentation.

Ownership and usage rights should be documented.

Externally sourced content must not be reproduced commercially without appropriate permission or a valid licence.

## 8. Bible Text and Translation Rights

The platform's use of Bible translations requires special consideration.

The initial prototype uses the King James Version.

Before commercial distribution, the project must verify the rights associated with the exact edition, source, transcription, or digital database being used.

The fact that an older Bible translation may be in the public domain does not automatically establish that every modern digital edition or transcription is free of restrictions.

Any additional translation must undergo a separate licensing review.

## 9. Third-Party Intellectual Property

The application may incorporate external software, fonts, icons, graphics, images, and other materials.

Each component must be reviewed under its applicable licence.

The project should maintain a record of:

- Provider or creator.
- Component name.
- Licence.
- Commercial-use permissions.
- Attribution requirements.
- Modification restrictions.
- Redistribution conditions.

Refer to:

`docs/THIRD_PARTY_SERVICES_AND_LICENSE_REGISTER.md`

## 10. Brand Protection and Consistency

Once approved, the brand identity should be used consistently across:

- Website.
- Android application.
- iOS application.
- Church display interface.
- Social media.
- Marketing materials.
- Promotional videos.
- Product documentation.

Unauthorized changes to official brand assets should be avoided.

A central collection of approved brand materials should be maintained.

## 11. Intellectual Property Risk Management

Potential risks include:

- Use of a name already registered by another organization.
- Unlicensed use of Bible content.
- Unauthorized reproduction of graphics.
- Disputes over developer-created software.
- Misuse of the application logo.
- Unauthorized copying or redistribution of original materials.

These risks should be reviewed before commercial release and whenever significant new content or branding is introduced.

## 12. Ownership and Contributor Agreements

Before accepting substantial contributions from outside developers, designers, writers, or other contractors, the project should establish appropriate written agreements.

Agreements should address:

- Ownership of contributed work.
- Permission to use and modify the contribution.
- Commercial distribution rights.
- Confidentiality where necessary.
- Third-party material incorporated into the work.
- Required attribution.

No contributor's ownership rights should be assumed to have transferred without an appropriate legal basis.

## 13. Commercial Launch Requirements

Before public commercial distribution:

- [ ] Product name and trademark availability reviewed.
- [ ] Domain name availability reviewed.
- [ ] Application store naming conflicts checked.
- [ ] Logo ownership and usage rights documented.
- [ ] Software ownership records maintained.
- [ ] Developer contribution agreements reviewed.
- [ ] Bible text and translation rights verified.
- [ ] Third-party licences reviewed.
- [ ] Marketing materials checked for unauthorized content.
- [ ] Brand assets organized and backed up.

## 14. Current Project Status

Revolving Bible is the current working product name.

The project is still in development.

Formal trademark registration, final logo approval, brand asset ownership agreements, and complete intellectual property clearance remain outstanding unless separately completed and documented.
