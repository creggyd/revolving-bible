## 1. Introduction

These Terms of Service establish the proposed conditions governing access to and use of the Revolving Bible platform.

The platform is designed to provide individuals and churches with digital Scripture tools, including automatically rotating Bible passages, daily readings, Scripture sharing, and church display management.

These terms are an internal planning draft. They must be reviewed by a qualified legal professional and adapted to the final operating business before being published or accepted by users.

## 2. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account or using services governed by these Terms, users will be required to acknowledge and accept the applicable terms.

Users who do not agree with the published terms should not create an account or use restricted services.

Where required by law, parental or guardian consent arrangements must be established before minors are permitted to use relevant account-based services.

## 3. Description of Services

The Revolving Bible platform is intended to provide the following services:

### 3.1 Individual Services

- Daily Scripture presentation.
- Automatically rotating Bible passages.
- Scripture favourites and saved passages.
- Scripture sharing.
- Reading preferences.
- Optional reminders and notifications.
- Future reading plans and reflection features.

### 3.2 Church Services

- Church Scripture display systems.
- Preacher-controlled Scripture readings.
- QR-code Scripture sharing.
- Synchronized display across multiple devices.
- Church administration tools.
- Service and Scripture scheduling.
- Authorized church user management.

### 3.3 Subscription Services

Some features may be offered through paid subscription plans.

The availability of free and paid features will be explained before users purchase or subscribe.

These services are planned features unless specifically identified as available in the released product.

## 4. User Accounts

Users may be required to create an account to access certain services.

Users are responsible for:

- Providing accurate registration information.
- Maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials.
- Protecting access to their accounts.
- Not sharing account access in ways that compromise security.
- Notifying the platform operator of suspected unauthorized access.

The platform operator reserves the right to investigate suspicious activity and take reasonable steps to protect user accounts.

## 5. Church Accounts and Administration

Church accounts may support multiple authorized users with different permission levels.

Church administrators will be responsible for:

- Managing authorized church members.
- Assigning appropriate administrative permissions.
- Maintaining accurate church information.
- Protecting administrative credentials.
- Removing access when a person is no longer authorized.

Church administrators must not use their permissions to access information belonging to another church or unauthorized account.

The platform will implement technical controls intended to separate information between church organizations.

## 8. Reflections and Educational Content

The platform may provide short Scripture reflections and related educational material.

Such material is intended to encourage personal reflection and Christian living.

Reflections will be developed using a church-neutral approach and will not be presented as an official interpretation endorsed by every Christian denomination.

Users should distinguish between the actual biblical text and supplementary commentary.

The platform is not a substitute for personal study, pastoral guidance, or independent theological judgment.

## 9. QR-Code Sharing

The platform may provide QR codes that allow users to access Scripture passages.

Users may share these QR codes for legitimate personal, church, and educational purposes.

QR codes must not be used to expose private account information, restricted administrative content, or information belonging to another user or church.

The platform operator may restrict or disable QR functionality where necessary for security or service protection.
