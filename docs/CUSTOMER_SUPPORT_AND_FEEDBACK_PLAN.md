# Customer Support and Feedback Management Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the proposed approach for providing customer support, managing reported issues, collecting feedback, and improving the Revolving Bible platform.

The objective is to provide reliable assistance to individual users, churches, preachers, and administrators while maintaining a consistent and professional support process.

## 2. Support Audiences

Support will be designed for the following user groups:

- Individual users of the Revolving Bible mobile application.
- Church administrators managing church accounts and displays.
- Preachers selecting and managing service readings.
- Display operators responsible for church screens and tablets.

## 3. Proposed Support Channels

The following channels are planned:

- **Support email:** A dedicated email address for customer enquiries and technical issues.
- **Online support form:** A form through which users can submit issues and feature requests.
- **Help centre:** Guides, frequently asked questions, troubleshooting instructions, and product updates.
- **In-app feedback:** A future feature allowing users to submit feedback directly from the application.

These channels are proposed and should not be presented as active services until implemented.

## 4. Support Categories

Support requests should be classified into the following categories:

1. Account registration and login.
2. Subscription and billing enquiries.
3. Scripture content and reference accuracy.
4. Automatic Scripture rotation.
5. Synchronisation between multiple devices.
6. QR code and Scripture-sharing functionality.
7. Preacher reading and church display management.
8. Church account access and permissions.
9. Application performance and compatibility.
10. Accessibility and general usability.
11. Feature requests and product suggestions.

## 5. Issue Reporting Requirements

When submitting a technical issue, users should be encouraged to provide:

- A brief description of the problem.
- The date and approximate time the problem occurred.
- The device type and operating system.
- The application version, where available.
- The steps taken before the issue occurred.
- A screenshot or screen recording, where appropriate.

Users must never be asked to submit passwords, authentication tokens, secret API keys, or other confidential account credentials.

## 6. Issue Priority Levels

Support requests will be assigned a priority based on their impact.

### Critical

A major service failure affecting the availability or security of the platform, or preventing many users from accessing essential services.

### High

A significant feature failure affecting a church display, Scripture synchronisation, account access, or another important function.

### Medium

A problem affecting an individual user or a non-critical feature where a reasonable workaround exists.

### Low

General enquiries, minor display issues, usability suggestions, and feature requests.

## 7. Proposed Response Targets

The following are initial planning targets, not guaranteed service commitments.

| Priority | Initial response target |
|---|---|
| Critical | Within 4 business hours |
| High | Within 1 business day |
| Medium | Within 2 business days |
| Low | Within 5 business days |

Actual support commitments will depend on staffing, operating costs, and the selected subscription plans.

## 8. Support Management Process

Each support request should follow a consistent process:

1. Receive the user's report.
2. Record the issue in a support tracking system.
3. Categorise and assign a priority.
4. Investigate and reproduce the issue where possible.
5. Communicate progress to the user.
6. Apply a fix or provide a suitable workaround.
7. Confirm the outcome with the user.
8. Close the request and record any lessons learned.

Unresolved technical issues should be escalated to the development or system administration team.

Security-related reports must be escalated promptly for investigation.

## 9. Feedback and Product Improvement

Customer feedback will be used to guide future development.

Feedback may be collected through:

- Church pilot programmes.
- User surveys.
- In-app feedback.
- Support requests.
- Feature suggestions.
- Discussions with church administrators and display operators.

Feedback should be reviewed periodically and considered when planning future releases.

## 10. Church Pilot Feedback

During initial church deployments, feedback should focus on:

- Ease of setting up the Scripture display.
- Readability from different areas of the church.
- Accuracy of displayed Scripture references.
- Synchronisation between multiple screens.
- Ease of selecting preacher readings.
- QR code accessibility.
- Reliability during church services.
- Overall user satisfaction.

Pilot feedback should be documented and used to identify improvements before wider commercial release.

## 11. Privacy and Information Handling

Support personnel must follow the platform's privacy and security requirements.

The support process should:

- Collect only information necessary to resolve an issue.
- Restrict access to customer support records.
- Avoid requesting confidential credentials.
- Protect screenshots and uploaded diagnostic information.
- Retain support records only for an approved period.
- Escalate suspected privacy or security incidents.

Customer information must not be used for unrelated purposes.

## 12. Support Performance Measurements

The following indicators should be monitored as the customer base grows:

- Number of support requests received.
- Average initial response time.
- Average issue resolution time.
- Number of unresolved issues.
- Frequency of recurring technical problems.
- Customer satisfaction.
- Feedback-driven improvements implemented.

These measurements will help identify service weaknesses and improve the customer experience.

## 13. Continuous Improvement

The support process should be reviewed regularly.

Common issues should be converted into troubleshooting articles or frequently asked questions where appropriate.

Recurring technical problems should be considered during software maintenance and future development planning.

## 14. Implementation Status

This document describes the planned support framework.

A dedicated support email, ticketing platform, help centre, and in-app feedback system have not yet been confirmed as implemented.

These services should be introduced in stages as the Revolving Bible platform progresses towards commercial launch.

## 15. Conclusion

A dependable customer support process is important to the long-term success of the Revolving Bible platform.

The proposed framework provides a foundation for assisting users, supporting churches, resolving technical problems, and using customer feedback to improve the product continuously.
